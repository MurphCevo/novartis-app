# System Overview - Project Autopilot

## What Is Project Autopilot?

Project Autopilot is a multi-agentic AI system that sits between Novartis Australia's marketing teams and their existing, disconnected marketing technology stack. It coordinates campaign content workflows - from knowledge discovery through content creation to asset management - using specialised AI agents accessed through a single natural-language chat interface.

The core thesis: AI handles coordination and drafting; humans direct, refine, and approve.

This is a Proof of Concept (Phase 1) scoped to three use cases and a unifying interface, designed to validate whether intelligent orchestration can reduce cycle time, cost, and compliance risk at a quality level the business trusts.

---

## The Problem It Solves

Novartis Australia's marketing operations for 70+ brands are constrained by:

| Constraint | Impact |
|---|---|
| Limited marketing capacity | 2 brand managers for 70+ brands - burnout, reactive planning |
| Fragmented processes | Manual, disconnected workflows - duplication, 14-week content cycles |
| Compliance complexity | Validation happens late - rework, non-compliance risk |
| Data silos | Scattered across 9+ systems - poor visibility, weak personalisation |
| Vendor dependence | ~$500k/yr agency spend, slow delivery |

The result is a 14-week end-to-end content supply chain that could be significantly compressed if the coordination burden were shifted from humans to AI.

## Actors

| Actor | Archetype | Role in System | Approximate Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing Manager | The Orchestrator | Primary user. Drives campaign strategy, uses Knowledge Discovery and Content Generation agents via chat. | ~25 |
| CRM Analyst | The Optimiser | Uses Knowledge Discovery for campaign performance data and audience insights. | (subset of above) |
| MLR Reviewer | Medical / Legal / Regulatory | Human-in-the-loop approval authority. Reviews AI-generated content and AI-suggested tags before commit. | ~5 |
| Digital Marketing Specialist | The Executor | Builds and publishes campaigns. Uses Content Generation for draft assembly, Asset Tagging for metadata consistency. | ~20 |

All actors interact with the system through the **Autopilot Assistant** - a single natural-language chat interface.

## What It Does (PoC Scope)

### Three Agents

| # | Agent | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| UC-4 | Knowledge Discovery | Semantic search across approved assets, strategy docs, and campaign performance data. Intent-based retrieval regardless of phrasing. |
| UC-5 | Content Generation | AI-drafted compliant copy and creative variants from approved templates, content blocks, and audience context. Human-in-the-loop review before publish. |
| UC-10 | Asset Tagging | Automatic metadata tagging based on content type, therapy area, audience, key message, and usage context. Write-back to source systems with approval. |

### One Interface

The **Autopilot Assistant** is the single entry point. It routes user intent to the appropriate agent(s), manages conversational context, and surfaces results.

### Nine Integration Targets

The system connects to Novartis's existing platforms (read and, for UC-10, write-back):

- Veeva Vault PromoMats (VVPM)
- Aprimo / Fuse
- SharePoint (post G-Drive migration)
- Sherlock (strategy and journey docs)
- Customer Data Hub / Snowflake
- Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC)
- Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
- Veeva Oncore CRM
- ShamanGo / Anthill Activator (modular templates)

## Governing Principles

These principles are non-negotiable and constrain all design decisions:

1. **Human-in-the-loop, always** - no content reaches a customer without a human decision.
2. **Compliance by design** - embed compliance at every stage, not just the review gate.
3. **Connect, don't replace** - Autopilot sits alongside existing tools, not in place of them.
4. **Prove before scaling** - every capability starts as a PoC with measurable criteria.
5. **Data readiness is a precondition** - we consume clean APIs; we don't absorb data integration debt.
6. **Ownership transfers to the client** - no black boxes, no vendor lock-in to Cevo.
7. **Quality over speed-to-demo** - optimise for reliability and auditability, not impressive first impressions.

---

## What It Is Not

- **Not a rip-and-replace** of any existing tool.
- **Not a full product** - it's a PoC validating the agent pattern before committing to scale.
- **Not a data platform** - Novartis provides clean APIs; Cevo integrates to them.
- **Not autonomous** - every action that affects external systems requires human approval.
- **Not trained on Novartis data** - base model training is prohibited by governance. Fine-tuning on approved copy is in scope (pending governance validation).
