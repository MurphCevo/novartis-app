## How to Use This Document

1. **Stakeholder alignment** — Share this matrix early to confirm "are these the right interactions?"
2. **Discovery prioritisation** — Start detailed discovery (using the [Interaction Pattern Template](32.01-interaction-pattern-template.md)) with High-risk systems first.
3. **Architecture decisions** — Once the "Agent Interface" column is filled, record each decision as an ADR in `33_decisions/`.
4. **Sprint planning** — Systems with Low readiness and Read-only patterns are candidates for early sprints. Write-back systems (VVPM, Aprimo, ShamanGo for UC-10) carry more integration risk and should be sequenced later.
