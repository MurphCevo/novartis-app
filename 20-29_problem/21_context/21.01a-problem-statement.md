# Problem Statement

> Back to [Conceptual Integrity](21.01-conceptual-integrity.md)

---

## The Problem

Novartis Australia's marketing operations for 70+ brands are bottlenecked by too few people, too many manual steps, and too many disconnected systems. The result is slow, expensive content delivery that struggles to stay compliant.

---

## Who Feels It

| Persona | Pain |
|---|---|
| Marketing Manager (2 for 70+ brands) | More time managing process than doing strategy. Reactive, not proactive. |
| CRM Analyst | No single source of truth. Fragmented customer data across systems. |
| Compliance Reviewer (MLR) | Everything lands at once. Manual referencing. Late-stage rework. |
| Digital Marketing Specialist | Juggling disconnected platforms. Slow, tedious publishing. |

---

## What's Broken

1. **Capacity gap**  - 2 brand managers cannot meaningfully serve 70+ brands. Work defaults to reactive execution.
2. **Fragmented workflow**  - Content moves through 7+ stages across disconnected tools with manual handoffs at every boundary.
3. **Compliance as a bottleneck**  - Validation is complex, manual, and happens too late in the process. Rework cycles extend timelines by weeks.
4. **Data is scattered**  - Campaign performance, customer insights, and approved assets live in separate systems with no unified view.
5. **Agency dependency**  - ~$500k/yr spent on external agencies for work that could be brought in-house with the right tooling.

---

## The Consequence

- 14-week end-to-end content cycle (from research to reporting)
- Missed opportunities for personalisation, lead nurture, and cross-channel integration
- Duplicated effort across brands and markets
- Compliance risk from manual, error-prone processes
- High cost per asset with limited ability to scale

---

## One-Line Summary

*Two people, seventy brands, fourteen weeks, and a stack of disconnected tools  - the maths doesn't work.*
