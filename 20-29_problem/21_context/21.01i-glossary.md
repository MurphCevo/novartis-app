Ubiquitous language  - terms that mean different things to different stakeholders. Prevents silent misalignment that survives every other note intact.

---

## Terms

| Term | Definition (as agreed for this project) | Where It Diverges | Source Stakeholder(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Autopilot Assistant | The AI chat interface through which marketers interact with agents in natural language. Not the full workbench. | Client vision doc uses "Autopilot" loosely for both assistant and workbench. | RFP, Appendix A |
| Autopilot Workbench | The Phase 2/3 web app  - a unified visual workspace. Out of scope for PoC. | Often conflated with the Assistant in conversation. | Appendix A |
| Agent / Skill | A single-purpose AI capability (e.g. Knowledge Discovery). An agent may have multiple skills. | "Agent" in Novartis context = the full orchestrated system; in our context = a discrete unit of work. | Cevo / Novartis |
| MLR | Medical, Legal, Regulatory  - the compliance review function. | Sometimes called "compliance" generically; MLR is the specific team/process. | Novartis |
| Modular content | Pre-approved content blocks that can be assembled into assets. Distinct from "templates." | Marketing teams sometimes use "template" and "module" interchangeably. | RFP §5b |
