## How to Use This

- Before designing a feature or making a trade-off, check these notes.
- If a decision conflicts with a guiding principle, surface it  - don't bury it. Log it in the Decision Record.
- If scope creeps beyond the boundary, it needs an explicit decision.
- At each discovery checkpoint (process mapping, data discovery, compliance discovery), ask: *does what we just learned confirm the CI, or force a revision?* Compliance discovery is the checkpoint most likely to force a real Guiding Principles or Scope Boundary change.
- A change to a **stable** note (Problem Statement, The Why) should be flagged to the client directly  - it usually means the original framing was incomplete, not just refined.
