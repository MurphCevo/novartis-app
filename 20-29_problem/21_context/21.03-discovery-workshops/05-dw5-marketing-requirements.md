# DW-5 - Marketing Requirements Discovery Workshop

**ClickUp:** 86d46977v | **Priority:** Core | **Duration:** 90-120 min
**Owner:** Cevo (discovery) + Novartis Marketing | **Feeds:** `31_reqs/`, UC-4 / UC-5 / UC-10

## Objective

Discover marketing requirements directly from the internal Novartis marketing team so the three PoC agents (Knowledge Discovery UC-4, Content Generation UC-5, Asset Tagging UC-10) and the Autopilot Assistant meet real user needs, at a quality bar the business trusts.

## Story

As the BA, I want to discover marketing requirements from the internal Novartis marketing team so that the agents (UC-4/5/10) meet real user needs.

## Attendees

- Cevo: BA (facilitator), SA
- Novartis: brand/marketing managers, CRM analyst, digital marketing specialist, content/creative lead

## Pre-reads

- Problem statement (`21.01a`) - personas
- Journey map (`21.02`) - content supply chain phases and OKRs
- Scope Boundary (`21.01d`) - the three in-scope agents

## Opening frame

> "We're designing three agents to support your work: one that finds knowledge and past campaign material, one that drafts compliant content from approved templates, and one that tags assets. Plus a single chat assistant to reach all three. Today is about how you actually work - the content you produce, the rules you work within, and what 'good' looks like - so we build something that fits you rather than the other way around."
