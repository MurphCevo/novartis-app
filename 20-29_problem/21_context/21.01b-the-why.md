# The Why

> Back to [Conceptual Integrity](21.01-conceptual-integrity.md)

---

## Why This Project

Novartis needs to shift marketing from manual coordination to intelligent orchestration. The current model  - agency-heavy, tool-fragmented, compliance-bottlenecked  - cannot scale to serve 70+ brands with a team of two.

Project Autopilot exists to prove that agentic AI can sit between marketers and their tools, handling the repetitive coordination so humans can focus on strategy, creativity, and oversight.

---

## Why Now

| Driver | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Capacity ceiling hit** | The team is already at burnout risk. Adding headcount doesn't solve a systemic workflow problem. |
| **AI readiness** | Agentic AI capabilities (multi-step orchestration, tool integration, natural language interfaces) have matured enough to attempt this. |
| **Cost pressure** | ~$500k/yr in agency spend for work that is routine, not creative. AI can absorb the routine. |
| **Compliance risk growing** | More brands, more content, same manual review process. The error surface is expanding. |
| **Platform transitions underway** | Several marketing tools are being replaced or upgraded  - a natural window to embed AI before workflows calcify around new systems. |
| **Competitive context** | Pharma peers are investing in AI-driven content operations. Inaction is itself a strategic choice. |

---

## Why Agentic AI (Not Just Another Tool)

The problem isn't a missing feature in one system. It's the disconnection *between* systems and the manual labour required to bridge them. A single-purpose tool would add to the fragmentation. An agentic layer that connects existing tools, applies compliance rules, and handles coordination addresses the systemic issue without ripping and replacing the stack.

---

## The Bet

If we can prove that AI agents reliably handle knowledge discovery, content generation, and asset tagging  - the three highest-readiness use cases  - then we have a foundation to extend into workflow orchestration, compliance intelligence, and personalisation across the full content supply chain.

Phase 1 is a proof-of-concept. The question it answers: *Can AI coordinate this work at a quality level humans trust and a speed the business needs?*
