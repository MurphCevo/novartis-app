Things currently treated as true but not yet validated — bets, not decisions. Distinct from the Decision Record: a decision is settled, an assumption is a checkable guess still in play.

## Notes

- Append new assumptions as they surface.
- When validated, update status to `confirmed`. When invalidated, update to `broken` and link to the decision or scope change that responds to it.
- Never delete — supersede with visible history.
