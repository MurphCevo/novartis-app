# Summary of Objectives

## Context

Novartis Australia's marketing operations for 70+ brands are bottlenecked by limited capacity (2 brand managers), fragmented manual workflows, and disconnected systems. The result is a 14-week content cycle, ~$500k/yr in agency spend, and growing compliance risk. Cevo has been engaged to design, build, and deliver a multi-agentic AI proof-of-concept (Project Autopilot) that proves intelligent orchestration can reduce cycle time, cost, and compliance risk at a quality level the business trusts.

---

## Objectives

| # | Objective | Maps to Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| OBJ.001 | Deliver a working Knowledge Discovery agent (UC4) that retrieves relevant strategy docs, past campaign performance, and approved assets from Novartis systems via natural language queries | DLV.001 |
| OBJ.002 | Deliver a working Content Generation agent (UC5) that drafts publish-ready, compliant marketing content from approved templates and content blocks, with human-in-the-loop review | DLV.002 |
| OBJ.003 | Deliver a working Asset Tagging agent (UC10) that automatically applies consistent metadata to assets aligned with the global metadata model and writes back to approved systems | DLV.003 |
| OBJ.004 | Deliver the Autopilot Assistant  - a single natural-language interface connecting marketers to all three PoC agents | DLV.004 |
| OBJ.005 | Validate system integration feasibility by connecting agents to Novartis tools (Aprimo/Fuse, Veeva Vault, Snowflake, SFMC, Sharepoint, etc.) via APIs or MCP servers | DLV.005 |
| OBJ.006 | Establish a governance-first architecture that meets Novartis compliance, data security, auditability, and AI governance requirements | DLV.006 |
| OBJ.007 | Produce a PoC evaluation report with measurable outcomes against agreed quality, performance, and end-user utility criteria to support Novartis's Go/No-Go decision on scale-up | DLV.007 |
| OBJ.008 | Transfer knowledge (architecture, operations, runbooks) to Novartis internal teams to reduce ongoing vendor dependency | DLV.008 |
