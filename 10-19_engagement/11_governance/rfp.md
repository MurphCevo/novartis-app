## 4. Project Overview

Our organisation is building a scalable, extensible multi-agentic AI solution for its marketing operations, from campaign conception to measurement and optimisation. Our goal is to create a cost-effective content supply chain, with an agentic-human-in-the-loop automated workflow.

### 4a. Background

Novartis's marketing operations for its long tail of 70+ brands are critically constrained by limited marketing capacity, manual workflows, and fragmented technology.

**Key challenges include:**

- Limited marketing capacity and budget to cover many brands
- Manual, fragmented workflows across departments and content planning, creation and publishing tools, leading to slow turnaround times
- No established approach or processes to support modular content, and the absence of templates to deliver modular content to customers
- Complex validation of content, with delays to go live, and risk of non-compliance
- Scattered data across tools, leading to:
  - Poor visibility to inform campaign planning, audience targeting strategy, and content planning (e.g. manual target setting for virtual field teams rather than potential-based allocation of resource)
  - Disconnected CX journeys across third party, virtual field team and digital channels

**These challenges result in:**

- High reliance on external agencies & reviewers, increasing the cost of marketing efforts and slowing time to market
- Missed opportunities to:
  - Create lead generation and nurture campaigns
  - Optimise content delivery with personalised customer experiences
  - Deliver integrated journeys across channels, particularly across virtual field team and digital channels, but also impacting integration of third party channel activity
  - Turn on meaningful marketing automation

Please see Appendix A for further context and detail.

**The opportunity** is to create a connected, intelligent marketing ecosystem that helps us launch the right campaigns across the right channels to the right audience.

### 4b. Deliverables and Services

We would like to engage the successful bidder to design, build, test, refine and deliver an AI assistant (AutoPilot Assistant) that connects marketers with the tools, data, and insights to simplify and strengthen our marketing efforts, using agentic AI. Our goal is to help teams work smarter, with AI doing the repetitive tasks while humans are in the loop directing the agents on next steps, and providing quality assurance.

Please see Appendix A for further information about our vision.

## 5. Project Scope

Create a multi-agentic AI solution for our proof-of-concept phase. All agents to be integrated to Novartis preferred marketing tools and relevant systems. Marketers to interact with agents in natural language via a single interface (Autopilot AI assistant).

### 5a. Project Objectives

1. Prove the value of a multi-agentic AI solution for campaign creation efficiency and speed to market
2. Prove that genAI can play a role in the creation of effective and compliant marketing materials for Novartis
3. Learn how we can train agents to integrate to and work effectively with Novartis preferred digital asset management, content authoring, and analytics tools so that we can transfer this knowledge into other workflow automation projects

### 5b. Functional Requirements

#### Use Case 4 — Knowledge Discovery (Campaign Design Assistance)

- A search experience that understands user intent and retrieves the right materials even when phrasing varies (e.g. searching "HCP adherence campaigns" or "congress summary templates" yields the same relevant content)
- End user can refine outputs using filters (market, channel, audience, asset type, time since creation, provenance)
- Retrieves relevant strategy and journey documents (Sherlock/Sharepoint)
- Retrieves past campaign performance and engagement data for similar/same audience targets (Customer Data Warehouse/Snowflake, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Google Analytics 4, Veeva Oncore CRM, 3rd party publishers)
- Retrieves relevant approved assets and/or modular content and templates from digital asset management systems (Sharepoint, Aprimo/Fuse, Veeva Vault Promomats, ShamanGo, Anthill Activator)
- Option for end user to "save to list" for use by agent for use case 5

#### Use Case 5 — Generate Publish-Ready Marketing Content

- More efficient content creation with easy reuse or adaption of approved assets within content authoring tools, and generation of new high-quality content based on audience insights and similar campaign performance where no approved assets exist
- Generate high-quality, compliant draft content (copy, imagery, or variants) directly from approved templates and content blocks where existing
- Generate relevant compliant draft content (copy, imagery and variants) suitable for specialised HCP audiences where no pre-approved templates or content blocks exist, with a quality similar to that achieved by the CoPilot Researcher agent (currently, prompts include campaign objectives, audience insights, desired output and length, and key claims)
- Human-in-the-loop review to refine and approve outputs within existing workflows, ensuring consistency and quality, without excessive reworking
- Seamless integration between AI generation and authoring tools, allowing end users to edit, version, and publish without manual transfer
- Draft assembly adheres to approved templates and constraints (e.g. required blocks included; structural rules applied)

#### Use Case 10 — Asset Tagging

- Automatic tagging for consistent, meaningful metadata based on content type, therapy area, audience, key message, and usage context for actionable insights, easy retrieval and campaign optimisation
- Recognises visual, textual, and contextual cues so that relevant tags are suggested or applied automatically in Novartis approved systems (Aprimo/Fuse, Veeva Vault Promomats, ShamanGo and Anthill Activator), aligned with the global metadata model
- Tagging rules and standards applied
- Human-in-the-loop review of tagging workflow before tags written back to Novartis systems

### 5c. Technical Requirements

- All solutions must be integrated to Novartis's development environment (GenAI Network, Azure, AWS) and/or approved AI workflow tools (Dify, GAIN, Microsoft). To support this outcome, the successful partner will work with Novartis teams for technical design and architecture, including model selection; data access and orchestration workflows; integration architecture; and Cloud infrastructure.
- Model access, capacity management and compliance controls in line with Novartis data governance policies.
- Continuous Integration and Continuous Development pipelines to automate build, deployment and update processes for Autopilot.
- LLM Observability tool and technical evaluation metrics to be included.
- AI Governance practices to be implemented as per the Risk and Data classification.
- Mechanism to monitor performance, user interactions and operational health.
- Role-based access and authentication for administrative, developer and end-user access to the platform.
- Knowledge/documentation of product developed and architecture will need to be transitioned and handed over to Novartis support when appropriate.

> **Note:** Novartis will also be responsible for connecting the successful bidder with the product owners for our Marketing technology platforms to build and test APIs for all new agents. Please note that these product owners are based across multiple time zones (depending on target system).

### 5d. Quality Requirements

During the proof-of-concept phase, all parts of the solution (agents and the Autopilot Assistant) must demonstrate:

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Quality** | Against an agreed test query set (relevance, coverage, consistency, accuracy, etc. depending on the agent) |
| **Governance filters** | Correct application of approval status; market; validity/expiry; workflow status etc., relevant to the agent |
| **Provenance** | Asset IDs/links, source references, claim blocks and/or rationale, depending on the agent |
| **Auditability** | Query and result logging, authoring (exact criteria dependent on the agent) |
| **Data security & privacy** | During model training and project application, data must not be used to train the base model |
| **End-user utility** | Qualitative feedback from end users will also be considered as part of the PoC success criteria |
| **Performance** | Latency within threshold and key NFRs to be fulfilled |

Novartis believes that these requirements are critical to our end goal of delivering compliant campaigns efficiently.

### 5e. Scale Up

Go/No-Go to scale up (full AutoPilot workbench development and creation of agents for remaining use cases in Appendix A) will be decided towards the end of the proof-of-concept phase based on agreed criteria.

Novartis may choose not to proceed to scale up if end-user utility is rated as low; if expected efficiencies are not realised in practice; or in case of change of strategic direction.
