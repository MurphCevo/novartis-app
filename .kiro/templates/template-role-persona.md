# Role Persona

**What it represents** - A functional position within an organisation. Position-centric; applies to anyone who holds the role, regardless of the individual.

**When to use:**

- RACI and responsibility assignment
- Service blueprinting and ITSM design
- Process ownership definition
- Enterprise architecture and capability mapping
- CCoE operating model design

**Key distinction from User Persona:** A role persona survives staff turnover. It describes what the role *does*, not who the person *is*.
